Make a companion

Put in a few pictures of the same thing, from empty to full, or one GIF. This page turns them into a companion: a scene that changes with a number.

Your pictures stay inside this browser. They are not sent anywhere.

Take it with you

The folder holds plain files: your pictures, a case table, and the runtime. Put it on a web host and open index.html?v=30&to=100 there. Opening the file by double-clicking does not work.

The five steps are the level condition in section 3.7 of the spec, and what you download is a companion made with a case table (section 7.1). More kinds of scenes are in Making a companion. The example pictures are the vase from the Tidepark gallery; they are not part of the download.
